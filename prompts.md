Arabic AI Productivity Toolkit

A collection of practical AI prompts designed to help Arabic-speaking users improve productivity and simplify everyday tasks.

1. Task Planning

Help me organize my daily tasks based on priority, urgency, and estimated completion time.

2. Content Writing

Act as a professional Arabic content writer and create clear, engaging, and well-structured content.

3. Learning Assistant

Explain complex topics in simple Arabic with practical examples and step-by-step instructions.

4. Business Assistant

Help small businesses improve their operations, marketing strategies, and customer communication using AI.

5. Research Assistant

Analyze a topic, identify important insights, and provide a structured summary.

Project Goals

* Make AI more accessible to Arabic-speaking users.
* Provide reusable and practical AI prompts.
* Encourage open-source collaboration.
* Continuously improve the quality of Arabic AI resources.
